# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: April 14, 2025_

Thank you for using **Status Saver** ("we", "our", or "us"). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application **Status Saver**.

## 1. Information We Collect

### Personal Information
We **do not collect or store** any personally identifiable information (PII) from our users.

### Storage Access
Our app requests permission to access your device's storage solely for the purpose of:

- Reading WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business statuses from the `/Android/media/` directory.
- Saving images and videos locally that you choose to download.

We do **not transmit** or upload your data to any external servers.

### Device Information
We may collect non-personal technical information (such as device model, OS version, and app version) for the purpose of debugging and improving app performance. This data is **anonymous and cannot be used to identify you**.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data to:
- Display and save statuses locally.
- Improve app functionality and user experience.
- Detect and notify you of new statuses (if enabled).

## 3. Permissions We Request

- **Storage Access (All Files / Media Access)**: Required to display and save statuses from your WhatsApp folders.
- **Notification Access** (Optional): Used to notify you when new statuses are available.

We only request the minimum necessary permissions required to offer full functionality.

## 4. Data Security

We are committed to protecting your data. All data stays **locally on your device** and is never shared with us or third-party servers.

## 5. Children’s Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from children.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
